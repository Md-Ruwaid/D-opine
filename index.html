Device Admin & Settings Shield

Restart & Boot Survival

ONEPLUS & OXYGENOS SETUP

How to Install on Android 14, 15 & 16

Modern Android versions require a one-time 30-second toggle to allow Accessibility services for sideloaded APKs.

01

Install the APK

Download the APK file directly to your device and tap it in your notifications or Files app to install.

02

Allow Restricted Settings

Go to Phone Settings -> Apps -> StrictLock -> Tap the 3 dots (top-right) -> Tap "Allow restricted settings" -> Enter your lock screen PIN.

03

Grant 6 Permissions

Open StrictLock, navigate to the Shields tab, and tap each permission button (Usage Access, Overlay, Accessibility, Device Admin, Battery Unrestricted).

04

Enable Auto-Launch

In Phone Settings -> Battery -> StrictLock -> Enable Allow background activity and Auto-launch so OxygenOS never kills the monitor.
